# China: reading notes (12 November 2026)

China's general law is the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), adopted 20 August 2021, in force
1 November 2021, not amended since (no amendment found on 12 November 2026; the Cybersecurity Law was
amended on 28 October 2025, in force 1 January 2026, on unrelated points). Read in Chinese on the official
CAC page (cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm) in the browser pane, all 74 articles; Arts. 66 to
71 (penalties) skimmed. The Chinese text is the only authentic text. English wording in the notice is the
builder's translation.

Also read on cac.gov.cn, in Chinese:
- Network Data Security Management Regulations (State Council Decree No. 790, 24 September 2024, in force
  1 January 2025), Arts. 2, 21 to 28 (personal information) and 34 to 39 (cross-border).
- Provisions on the Cyber Protection of Children's Personal Information (CAC Order No. 4, 22 August 2019,
  in force 1 October 2019), Arts. 2, 8 to 10.
- CAC announcement of 18 July 2025 on reporting personal information protection officers (threshold).
- Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-Border Data Flows (CAC Order No. 16, 22 March 2024):
  Arts. 5 and 8 only as quoted on the CAC page and in search extracts; not read in full.
Not read: the Standard Contract Measures (2023), the Security Assessment Measures (2022), the outbound
certification measures in force 1 January 2026, the compliance audit measures (2025), GB/T 35273 (a
recommended standard, revision drafted June 2026) and GB/T 45574-2025 (sensitive personal information,
recommended). None is needed for the content of the notice; they govern the transfer mechanism.

## PIPL
- Art. 3: applies in China; outside China where the purpose is to provide products or services to
  natural persons in China, or to analyse or assess their behaviour, or as laws provide.
- Art. 4: personal information excludes anonymised information; processing includes collection, storage,
  use, processing, transmission, provision, disclosure, deletion.
- Arts. 5 to 7: lawful, legitimate, necessary, good faith; clear and reasonable purpose, minimum scope;
  openness and transparency: "公开个人信息处理规则，明示处理的目的、方式和范围".
- Art. 13 (grounds): (1) consent; (2) necessary to conclude or perform a contract to which the person is a
  party, or for human resources management under lawful labour rules and collective contracts; (3)
  statutory duties or obligations; (4) public health emergencies, or protecting life, health and property
  in an emergency; (5) news reporting and public opinion supervision in the public interest, within a
  reasonable scope; (6) information the person disclosed or otherwise lawfully disclosed, within a
  reasonable scope; (7) other cases in laws and administrative regulations. Second paragraph: where other
  provisions require consent, it is not needed in cases (2) to (7).
- Art. 14: consent informed, voluntary, explicit; separate or written consent where law requires; a
  change of purpose, method or categories needs new consent.
- Art. 15: right to withdraw; a convenient way to withdraw; earlier processing unaffected.
- Art. 16: no refusal of products or services because the person refuses or withdraws consent, unless the
  processing is necessary to provide them.
- Art. 17, verbatim: "个人信息处理者在处理个人信息前，应当以显著方式、清晰易懂的语言真实、准确、完整地向个人
  告知下列事项：（一）个人信息处理者的名称或者姓名和联系方式；（二）个人信息的处理目的、处理方式，处理的个人信息种类、
  保存期限；（三）个人行使本法规定权利的方式和程序；（四）法律、行政法规规定应当告知的其他事项。" Changes:
  tell the person what changed. Processing rules used to inform must be public and easy to consult and
  save.
- Art. 18: no notice where laws require confidentiality or no notice; in an emergency, notice afterwards.
- Art. 19: shortest retention necessary for the purpose, unless laws provide otherwise.
- Art. 20: joint processors agree their rights and duties; the person can exercise rights against any;
  joint liability.
- Art. 21: entrusted processing (processors) by agreement; supervision; no sub-entrustment without consent.
- Art. 22: transfer on merger, division, dissolution or bankruptcy: tell the person the recipient's name
  and contact; a change of purpose or method needs new consent.
- Art. 23: providing personal information to another processor: tell the person the recipient's name,
  contact, purpose, method and categories, and obtain separate consent.
- Art. 24: automated decision-making: transparency, fair results, no unreasonable differential treatment
  in prices; for information push and commercial marketing, an option not targeted at personal
  characteristics, or a convenient way to refuse; for decisions with a major impact, a right to an
  explanation and to refuse decisions made solely by automated means.
- Art. 25: no public disclosure without separate consent. Art. 26: image capture in public places, signs.
  Art. 27: publicly available information within a reasonable scope; consent where the impact is major.
- Art. 28: sensitive personal information: biometrics, religious belief, specific identity, medical health,
  financial accounts, whereabouts, and personal information of minors under 14; only with a specific
  purpose, sufficient necessity and strict protection.
- Art. 29: separate consent for sensitive personal information (written where law requires).
- Art. 30: for sensitive personal information, also tell the person the necessity and the impact on their
  rights and interests.
- Art. 31: under 14: consent of the parents or other guardians; dedicated processing rules.
- Arts. 33 to 37: State organs (notice under Art. 35 with exceptions; storage in China).
- Art. 38: outbound transfer conditions: CAC security assessment, certification, the CAC standard
  contract, or other conditions in laws, regulations or CAC rules; treaties.
- Art. 39: for outbound provision, tell the person the overseas recipient's name, contact, purpose, method,
  categories, and how to exercise PIPL rights against the overseas recipient, and obtain separate consent.
- Art. 40: CIIOs and processors above the CAC volume store in China; security assessment.
- Art. 41: no provision to foreign judicial or law enforcement bodies without approval.
- Art. 44: right to know and decide, and to restrict or refuse processing (unless laws provide otherwise).
- Art. 45: access and copy, promptly; portability to a designated processor where CAC conditions are met.
- Art. 46: correction and supplementing. Art. 47: deletion (purpose achieved, service stopped or retention
  ended, consent withdrawn, unlawful processing, others); where retention is required or deletion is
  technically difficult, stop all processing except storage and security measures.
- Art. 48: explanation of the processing rules. Art. 49: next of kin of a deceased person, for their own
  lawful interests, can exercise access, copy, correction and deletion, unless the deceased arranged
  otherwise.
- Art. 50: a convenient mechanism for requests; reasons for refusal; the person can sue in a people's
  court.
- Art. 52: processors above the CAC volume appoint a personal information protection officer; publish
  the officer's contact and report name and contact to the authorities.
- Art. 53: processors outside China under Art. 3(2) set up a dedicated entity or appoint a representative
  in China and report its name and contact.
- Arts. 54 to 57: compliance audits, impact assessments (sensitive, automated decisions, entrusting,
  providing, disclosing, outbound), breach notices.
- Art. 58: large platforms. Arts. 60 to 65: CAC coordinates; departments accept complaints and reports;
  Art. 65: anyone can complain or report unlawful processing; the departments publish their channels.
- Art. 72: personal or household affairs excluded. Art. 73: definitions (processor; automated
  decision-making; de-identification; anonymisation).

## Network Data Security Management Regulations (in force 1 January 2025)
- Art. 21, verbatim opening: where a processor informs through processing rules, the rules shall be
  "集中公开展示、易于访问并置于醒目位置，内容明确具体、清晰易懂" and include at least: (1) name and contact;
  (2) purposes, methods and categories, the necessity of sensitive personal information and the impact on
  rights; (3) retention period and handling after expiry, or the method for determining it; (4) the methods
  and channels to access, copy, transfer, correct, supplement, delete, restrict processing, deregister an
  account and withdraw consent. Purposes, methods and categories of collection and of provision to other
  processors, with recipient information, are listed in a list ("以清单等形式予以列明"). Under 14:
  dedicated processing rules.
- Art. 22: consent rules (necessary for the product or service; separate consent for sensitive; guardian
  under 14; no processing beyond consent; no frequent re-asking after a refusal; new consent for changes).
- Art. 23: accept and handle requests promptly, convenient channels, no unreasonable conditions.
- Art. 24: delete or anonymise on account deregistration (or stop all but storage and security).
- Art. 25: portability conditions: identity verified; data provided with consent or collected under a
  contract; technically feasible; no harm to others; a fee for excessive requests.
- Art. 26: report the Art. 53 entity or representative to the city-level CAC office.
- Art. 28: processors of personal information of 10 million or more people follow Arts. 30 and 32.
- Art. 35: outbound personal information allowed where: security assessment; certification; the standard
  contract; necessary for a contract with the person; cross-border human resources management;
  statutory duties; emergencies; other conditions.

## Children (CAC Order No. 4, 2019)
- Art. 2: a child is a minor under 14. Art. 8: dedicated rules and user agreement; a designated person.
  Art. 9: tell the guardian prominently and clearly, and obtain the guardian's consent. Art. 10: with a
  refusal option, tell the guardian: purposes, methods and scope; storage location, period and handling
  after expiry; security measures; consequences of refusing; complaint channels; how to correct and delete;
  a material change needs consent again.

## Personal information protection officer
CAC announcement of 18 July 2025 (cac.gov.cn/2025-07/18/c_1754553420421538.htm): processors of personal
information of 1 million or more people report their officer to the city-level CAC office (PIPL Art. 52;
Compliance Audit Measures Art. 12); online system grxxbh.cacdtsc.cn.

## Cross-border (Provisions of 22 March 2024, as quoted)
Art. 5: exempt from the security assessment, the standard contract and certification: (1) contracts with
the person (shopping, delivery, payment, account opening, travel and hotel bookings, visas, exams); (2)
cross-border HR management; (3) emergencies; (4) a processor other than a CIIO that provides personal
information of fewer than 100,000 people (no sensitive information) abroad since 1 January of the year.
Art. 8: 100,000 to 1 million people (no sensitive), or fewer than 10,000 people's sensitive information:
standard contract or certification. Above: security assessment (Art. 7). The Art. 39 notice and consent
rules still apply.

## What the notice therefore needs (Part Q)
Contents: the processor's name and contact; purposes, methods, categories and retention, with handling
after expiry; the methods and procedures for each right (access, copy, portability, correction,
supplementing, deletion, restriction or refusal, explanation, withdrawal, account deregistration, next of
kin); for sensitive personal information its necessity and impact; for provision to another processor and
for overseas recipients, each recipient's name, contact, purpose, method and categories (and for overseas
recipients how to exercise rights against them), with separate consent where consent is the ground; the
officer's contact (1 million people); the representative in China for processors abroad (Art. 53, good
practice to publish); under 14 dedicated rules; automated decision-making choices; changes and transfers on
merger. Language: clear and easily understood, which for people in China is normally Chinese; the
generator writes English only.
