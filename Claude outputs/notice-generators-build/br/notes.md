# Brazil: reading notes (12 November 2026)

## LGPD, Lei 13.709/2018, consolidated text
Source: normas.leg.br (Senado Federal, LexML), structured text via its public API
(`/api/public/normas?urn=urn:lex:br:federal:lei:2018-08-14;13709&tipo_documento=maior-detalhe`),
dateModified 2026-02-25. planalto.gov.br refused automated access ("Acesso Negado"). Read in Portuguese;
paraphrased in English below. Amendments after 2019 touch only the ANPD's institutional articles: MP
1.317/2025 converted into Lei 15.352/2026 (25 February 2026) made the ANPD the "Agencia Nacional de
Protecao de Dados" (Art. 5, VIII and XIX as amended).

- Art. 9 (access to information about processing, clear, adequate and prominent, among other
  characteristics set by regulation): I specific purpose; II form and duration (trade secrets
  respected); III controller identity; IV controller contact information; V information on shared use
  of data by the controller and its purpose; VI responsibilities of the agents who will carry out the
  processing; VII the data subject's rights, with explicit mention of the Art. 18 rights.
  Para. 1: where consent is required, it is void if the information was misleading or abusive or not
  presented beforehand transparently, clearly and unequivocally. Para. 2: where consent is required and
  the purpose changes incompatibly, inform beforehand; the person can revoke. Para. 3: where processing
  is a condition for a product, service or the exercise of a right, the person is told prominently, and
  told how to exercise the Art. 18 rights.
- Art. 8: consent in writing (a highlighted clause) or other means showing the will; burden of proof on
  the controller; determined purposes, generic authorisations void; Para. 5 revocable at any time by
  express request, free and facilitated procedure; Para. 6 a change to Art. 9 I, II, III or V must be
  communicated with specific highlight of its content; where consent is required the person can revoke.
- Art. 7 legal bases: I consent; II legal or regulatory obligation; III public administration for
  public policies; IV studies by a research body (anonymised where possible); V performance of a
  contract or preliminary procedures at the data subject's request; VI regular exercise of rights in
  judicial, administrative or arbitral proceedings; VII protection of life or physical safety of the
  data subject or a third party; VIII health protection, exclusively by health professionals, services
  or sanitary authority; IX legitimate interests of the controller or a third party, unless the data
  subject's fundamental rights and freedoms prevail; X credit protection. Para. 5: a controller that
  obtained consent and needs to communicate or share data with other controllers needs specific consent
  for that, save the exemptions.
- Art. 10: legitimate interest only for legitimate purposes from concrete situations (I support and
  promotion of the controller's activities; II protection of the data subject's regular exercise of
  rights or services that benefit them); Para. 1 strictly necessary data; Para. 2 the controller must
  adopt measures to ensure transparency of processing based on legitimate interest.
- Art. 11 sensitive data bases: I specific and highlighted consent for specific purposes; II without
  consent where indispensable for: a legal or regulatory obligation; b shared processing by public
  administration for public policies; c studies by a research body; d regular exercise of rights,
  including in a contract and in proceedings; e protection of life or physical safety; f health
  protection by health professionals; g fraud prevention and security of the data subject in
  identification and authentication processes in electronic systems (Art. 9 rights preserved, unless
  fundamental rights prevail). Art. 5, II sensitive data: racial or ethnic origin, religious belief,
  political opinion, membership of a trade union or a religious, philosophical or political
  organisation, health or sex life, genetic or biometric data when linked to a natural person.
- Art. 14 children and adolescents: best interest; Para. 1 children: specific and highlighted consent
  of at least one parent or legal guardian; Para. 2 keep public the types of data collected, how they
  are used and the procedures to exercise Art. 18 rights; Para. 6 information simple, clear and
  accessible, adapted to the user, audiovisual where appropriate, for parents and understandable by the
  child.
- Art. 16: deletion after processing ends; retention allowed for I legal or regulatory obligation;
  II research; III transfer to a third party respecting the Law; IV exclusive use of the controller,
  anonymised.
- Art. 18 rights (at any time, on request): I confirmation of processing; II access; III correction of
  incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data; IV anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary,
  excessive or unlawfully processed data; V portability to another provider, on express request, per
  ANPD regulation (trade secrets); VI deletion of data processed with consent (except Art. 16); VII
  information on the public and private entities with which the controller shared data; VIII
  information on the possibility of refusing consent and the consequences; IX revocation of consent
  (Art. 8 Para. 5). Para. 1 petition the national authority against the controller; Para. 8 also before
  consumer protection bodies. Para. 2 object to processing based on a basis other than consent, in case
  of non-compliance with the Law. Para. 3 by express request of the data subject or a legally
  constituted representative. Para. 4 if not immediately possible: say it is not the agent (and name
  it where possible) or give the reasons. Para. 5 free of charge, within the time limits of regulation.
  Para. 6 tell agents with whom data was shared about corrections, deletions etc.
- Art. 19: confirmation or access I in simplified form, immediately; or II a clear and complete
  declaration (origin of the data, absence of records, criteria used, purpose) within 15 days of the
  request. Para. 2 electronically or in print, at the data subject's choice. Para. 3 where processing is
  based on consent or contract, a full electronic copy in a reusable format, per ANPD regulation.
- Art. 20: right to request review of decisions taken solely on automated processing that affect the
  data subject's interests, including profiling (personal, professional, consumer, credit profile or
  aspects of personality). Para. 1 on request, clear and adequate information on the criteria and
  procedures used (trade secrets). Para. 2 the ANPD can audit for discrimination.
- Art. 23, I (public bodies): inform the cases in which they process personal data, with the legal
  basis, purpose, procedures and practices, preferably on their websites.
- Art. 33 international transfers only: I to countries or bodies with an adequate level of protection;
  II with guarantees: a specific contractual clauses, b standard contractual clauses, c global corporate
  rules, d seals, certificates and codes of conduct; III international legal cooperation between public
  intelligence, investigation and prosecution bodies; IV protection of life or physical safety; V ANPD
  authorisation; VI international cooperation agreement; VII public policy or legal attribution of a
  public service (with Art. 23 publicity); VIII specific and highlighted consent with prior information
  on the international character, clearly distinguished from other purposes; IX Art. 7 II, V or VI.
- Art. 41: the controller must appoint an encarregado (data protection officer); Para. 1 identity and
  contact details disclosed publicly, clearly and objectively, preferably on the controller's website;
  Para. 2 tasks; Para. 3 the ANPD can set exemptions by nature, size or volume.
- Art. 6, VI transparency principle: clear, precise and easily accessible information on the processing
  and the processing agents.

## ANPD regulations read (12 November 2026)
- Res. CD/ANPD 18/2024 (encarregado), DOU 17 July 2024. Art. 8: disclose and keep up to date the
  encarregado's identity and contact information. Art. 9: publicly, clearly and objectively, in a
  prominent and easily accessible place on the agent's website; without a website, by other means
  usually used to contact data subjects (Para. 3). Identity at minimum: full name of a natural person;
  for a legal entity, its business name or trade name plus the full name of the natural person
  responsible. Contact information at minimum: means that allow data subjects to exercise their rights
  with the controller and allow the ANPD to send communications. The encarregado may be an employee, an
  external natural person or a legal entity. Source: DOU print hosted on tjba.jus.br and the TSE
  clipping of DOU 17/07/2024 p. 42; law-firm summaries agree (Mattos Filho, legismap).
- Res. CD/ANPD 2/2022 (small processing agents), DOU 28 January 2022 (print hosted by gov.br/palmares).
  Art. 2 small agents: micro and small companies (LC 123/2006), startups (LC 182/2021), private legal
  entities including non-profits, and natural persons or unincorporated entities acting as agents.
  Art. 3: not if high-risk processing (Art. 4: one general criterion, large scale or significant
  effect, plus one specific criterion: emerging technology, surveillance of public-access areas, solely
  automated decisions including profiling, or sensitive data or data of children, adolescents or
  older people), turnover above the LC thresholds, or part of a group above them. Art. 7: information
  under LGPD Arts. 9 and 18 by electronic, printed or any other means. Art. 11: no obligation to
  appoint an encarregado; Para. 1 must then provide a communication channel with data subjects;
  Para. 2 appointing one counts as good practice. Art. 14: double time for data subject requests,
  incident notices (unless physical or moral integrity or national security), the Art. 19 II full
  declaration (so 30 days) and ANPD requests. Art. 15: the Art. 19 I simplified declaration within up
  to 15 days.
- Res. CD/ANPD 19/2024, International Data Transfer Regulation (English version published by the
  ANPD). Art. 3 V and Art. 6: international collection (data collected directly from the data subject
  by an agent abroad) is not an international transfer; the LGPD may still apply under its Art. 3.
  Art. 9: purposes legitimate, specific, explicit and informed to the data subject; a legal basis
  under Art. 7 or 11 plus a mechanism: (a) ANPD adequacy decision; (b) standard contractual clauses,
  global corporate rules or specific contractual clauses; (c) LGPD Art. 33 II d and III to IX.
  Art. 16: SCCs adopted in full and unmodified (Annex II); the 12-month incorporation deadline (23
  August 2025) has passed. Art. 17 caput and Para. 1: give the data subject the full text of the
  clauses on request within 15 days (trade secrets respected). Art. 17 Para. 2: publish on the website
  a document in Portuguese, in simple, clear, precise and accessible language, with at least: I form,
  duration and specific purpose of the transfer; II destination country; III controller
  identification and contact; IV shared use and its purpose; V agents' responsibilities and security
  measures; VI data subject rights and how to exercise them, including an easily accessible channel and
  the right to petition the ANPD against the controller. Para. 3: on a specific page or integrated,
  prominently and easily accessible, in the privacy policy. Art. 32: the same for specific clauses and
  global corporate rules, subject to the approval decision.
- Res. CD/ANPD 32/2026 (26 January 2026): the European Union recognised as adequate (Art. 33 I).
  Corrected after the independent review (R01): Article 1, sole paragraph covers all EU Member States,
  the three EFTA States in the EEA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway) and EU institutions, bodies and
  agencies; Article 2 excludes transfers made only for public security, national defence, State
  security or criminal investigation. The builder's first reading, from secondary sources, missed the
  EEA States. Article 1 confirmed in the Portuguese text reproduced by legismap (DOU 27.01.2026 p. 49)
  and the ANPD's Spanish version.
- Data subject rights regulation (Arts. 9, 18, 19, 20 and 23): on the ANPD Regulatory Agenda 2025-2026,
  phase 1; internal consultation May-June 2025 and an AGU opinion in 2025; no adopted resolution found
  as of 12 November 2026 (the ANPD's list of regulations, read through WebFetch the same day, shows only
  Resolution 32/2026 for 2026). Deadlines therefore stay as in the Law (Art. 19) and Res. 2/2022. Open
  point.
- ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025, in force 17 March 2026): a separate child online safety law for IT
  products and services aimed at or likely accessed by children. Not a privacy notice law; listed as a
  limit, like the US children's codes.

## Design decisions
- Regime "br", after "ch" in REG_ORDER. Lead (whole document) or section ("Additional information for
  people in Brazil").
- Legal basis per purpose (Art. 7, I to X) and, where sensitive data is used, the Art. 11 basis.
  Answers are per law and never reused from GDPR or Switzerland.
- Encarregado: name (natural person) or business name plus responsible person, and contact; or the
  small agent route: no encarregado, a communication channel instead (Res. 2/2022 Art. 11).
- Rights: the nine Art. 18 rights, petition to the ANPD and to consumer bodies, objection (Para. 2),
  response times (simplified immediately, full within 15 days; 15 and 30 days for small agents),
  free of charge, review of automated decisions and the criteria on request (Art. 20).
- Consent: free and facilitated revocation (Art. 8 Para. 5), the possibility to refuse consent and its
  consequences (Art. 18 VIII), specific consent for sharing (Art. 7 Para. 5).
- Condition of a service (Art. 9 Para. 3): prominent statement.
- Shared use (Art. 9 V) and responsibilities of agents (Art. 9 VI).
- International transfers: countries and mechanism; with SCCs, the Art. 17 Para. 2 items and the
  15-day clause request; the transfer section must be in Portuguese (the generator warns, it does not
  translate).
- Children (Art. 14): parent or guardian consent, public information on data types, use and how to
  exercise rights, simple language.
- Public bodies (Art. 23 I): legal basis, purpose, procedures and practices.
- Changes (Art. 8 Para. 6, Art. 9 Para. 2): specific highlight; revoke consent if the person disagrees.
- Language: the LGPD does not require Portuguese for the notice itself, but the Consumer Defence Code
  (Art. 31) requires information in Portuguese for consumer offers, and Res. 19/2024 requires it for
  the transfer document. The generator writes English and warns in the checks.
