# Japan: reading notes (12 November 2026)

Japan's general law is the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI, 個人情報の保護に関する法律,
Act No. 57 of 2003). Read in Japanese on e-Gov (laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/415AC0000000057), the version in
force on 2 November 2026 (amended by Act No. 62 of 2026, the Disaster Management Agency law; e-Gov marks
unenforced amendments). The Japanese text is the only authentic text; the PPC's English translation was
not used. Also read on e-Gov: the Enforcement Order (Cabinet Order No. 507 of 2003, laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/
415CO0000000507), Arts. 2, 10 to 13; the Enforcement Rules (PPC Rules No. 3 of 2016, in force 14 June
2026, laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/428M60020000003), Arts. 15 to 18. PPC Notice No. 1 of 2019 (as revised
18 April 2023, ppc.go.jp/files/pdf/h31kokuji1_rev2023April18.pdf) through a web fetch: the designated
countries are the EEA states listed and the United Kingdom. The PPC general guidelines (通則編,
ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/guidelines_tsusoku/) through a web fetch, sections 3-1-1, 3-6-2-1, 3-8-1,
3-9 and 10-7 only.

## 2026 amendment (pending)
The Act to amend the APPI (Act No. 56 of 2026) was passed on 10 July 2026 and promulgated on 17 July
2026; in principle in force on a date set by Cabinet Order within two years of promulgation (PPC page
ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/r8kaiseihogohou; PPC outline 260407_gaiyou.pdf, read through a web fetch).
Notice-relevant points in the outline: for a person under 16, consent and notices go to the legal
representative; certain matters on handling facial feature data must be made known; consent waived for
statistics (including AI development that counts as statistics); entrustment duties revised; breach
notice to individuals relaxed. Penalties partly from 17 January 2027 (law firm newsletter, secondary).
Not modelled until the Cabinet Order, Rules and guidelines are out; row T18 says so.

## APPI provisions that shape the notice
- Art. 2(3), Order Art. 2: special care-required personal information (要配慮個人情報): race, creed,
  social status, medical history, criminal record, having been a victim of crime, disabilities, health
  check results, guidance and treatment, criminal and juvenile proceedings.
- Art. 17(1): purposes of use as specific as possible; (2) change only within a scope reasonably
  related to the original. Guidelines 3-1-1: where information is analysed for behaviour or interests,
  the purpose must let the person predict that handling.
- Art. 18(1): beyond the purposes only with prior consent; (3) exceptions (law; life, body, property
  and consent hard to get; public health or children; cooperation with State; academic).
- Art. 20(2): special care-required information acquired only with prior consent, unless (i) to (viii).
- Art. 21(1): on acquisition, notify or publish the purpose unless published in advance; (2) direct
  acquisition in writing (contract and other documents, electronic included): state the purpose
  expressly in advance; (3) changed purpose notified or published; (4) exceptions.
- Art. 22: keep accurate, and try to delete without delay when no longer needed.
- Art. 23, 25: security measures; supervision of entrusted parties.
- Art. 27(1): no provision to third parties without prior consent, except (i) law to (vii) academic;
  (2) opt-out provision with items (name, address, representative; provision as purpose; items; method
  of acquisition; method of provision; stopping on request; how requests are received; others in Rules)
  notified to the person or easily knowable, and filed with the PPC; not for special care-required data
  or data itself received by opt-out; (5) not third parties: (i) entrustment, (ii) succession, (iii)
  joint use with notice of: the fact, items, scope of joint users, their purposes, and the name, address
  and representative of the person responsible; (6) changes.
- Art. 28(1): to a third party in a foreign country: prior consent to provision to a third party in a
  foreign country, except where the country is designated (Rules Art. 15; PPC Notice No. 1 of 2019: EEA
  and UK) or the recipient has a system meeting Rules Art. 16 (contract or other appropriate and
  reasonable method ensuring measures in line with Chapter 4 Section 2; or recognition under an
  international framework, such as APEC CBPR); (2) before consent, information (Rules Art. 17: the name
  of the country, its system for personal information protection obtained by an appropriate and
  reasonable method, the recipient's measures; if the country is not identifiable, that fact, why, and
  other helpful information); (3) for system recipients: necessary measures (Rules Art. 18(1): periodic
  checks) and information on request (Rules Art. 18(3) items).
- Art. 32(1): put in a state the person can know (including answering without delay on request): (i)
  name, address and, for a corporation, the representative's name; (ii) all purposes of use of
  retained personal data; (iii) procedures for requests (notice of purpose, disclosure, correction,
  suspension) with any fee; (iv) Order Art. 10: (1) security measures taken (except those whose
  disclosure would hinder security), (2) where to make complaints, (3) for a member of an accredited
  personal information protection organisation, its name and complaint contact. (2), (3) notice of the
  purpose on request. Guidelines 3-8-1: "the state the person can know" means they can know it if they
  want to; a statement that the business follows the guidelines is not enough; categories of measures:
  basic policy, rules, organisational, human, physical, technical, understanding the external
  environment (10-7: where personal data is handled in a foreign country, know its system; the
  country's name in a form the person can reasonably recognise).
- Art. 33: disclosure, electronically or by another method the person asks for; (5) records of
  provision to third parties. Art. 34: correction, addition or deletion when content is untrue. Art. 35:
  suspension or erasure (handled beyond purpose, inappropriate use, improper acquisition); stop provision
  to third parties (in breach of Arts. 27(1), 28); (5) also when no longer needed, after a reportable
  breach, or where rights or legitimate interests are at risk. Art. 36: reasons. Art. 37, Order Art. 12:
  procedures (where, form, identity check, fee collection); agents. Art. 38: fees for purpose notice and
  disclosure, reasonable in view of actual costs. Art. 39: two-week prior request before a lawsuit.
- Art. 40: complaints handled appropriately and promptly.
- Art. 171: applies to a business abroad handling, in connection with goods or services to persons in
  Japan, the personal information of persons in Japan.
- Art. 31: personal related information (cookies) provided to a third party that will acquire it as
  personal data: the provider confirms the person's consent. Modelled after the review (rows T25, T27).

## What the notice therefore needs (Part R)
Name, address and representative; all purposes, specific; special care-required information and its
consent; third parties with the basis (consent, law, exception), joint use items, opt-out items;
third parties in foreign countries with the basis, and for consent the country, its system and the
recipient's measures; how requests are made, with any fee; the security measures (with the foreign
countries where data is handled); the complaint contact; the accredited organisation if a member; how
purposes change. Retention is not required (Art. 22 asks for deletion efforts). The language is not
set by the APPI; Japanese is expected for people in Japan.

## After the independent review (12 November 2026)
Read in Japanese on e-Gov in the browser pane after the review: APPI Art. 2(11) (administrative organs etc.,
Annex 2 bodies excluded from incorporated administrative agencies), Art. 16(2) (businesses exclude the
State, local governments, incorporated administrative agencies and local incorporated administrative
agencies), Art. 27(2) proviso and items (i) to (viii) (item (i) covers the representative or administrator
of an association without legal personality), Art. 27(3) (changes to item (i) and stopping: without delay;
changes to items (iii) to (v), (vii), (viii): in advance; notify or make easily knowable, and file),
Art. 31(1) to (3) (consent of the person to the recipient acquiring the information as personal data; for
a recipient abroad, information on the country's system and the recipient's measures given beforehand under
the Rules; Art. 28(3) and the confirmation rules applied), Arts. 32(3), 33(2) (the chosen method, or paper
where it is very costly or difficult), 33(3), 33(5) (the records under Arts. 29(1) and 30(3)), 35(7), 36
(a duty to try to explain refusals), 41(4) (purposes of pseudonymously processed information published, not
notified), 41(6) (no provision to third parties except under law; joint use published), 43(3), (4) (items
published; when provided, items and method published and the recipient told it is anonymously processed
information), 58(1), (2). Enforcement Order Art. 9 ((i) evident from appearance when seen or filmed; (ii)
received on entrustment, business succession or joint use) and Art. 12 (items (i) to (iv), (iv) the method
of collecting the fee). Enforcement Rules Art. 11(1) (time for the person to ask for the provision to stop),
11(4) (update method; planned start date), Art. 17 (also governs Art. 31(1)(ii); (3) country not
identifiable: that fact, why, and helpful information; (4) measures not describable: that fact and why),
Art. 18. PPC general guidelines through a web fetch: 2-15 (publication; the example of a page about one
click from the top page), 2-16 (consent from a parent or legal representative where a minor cannot judge),
3-6-3 (the scope of joint users clear enough for the person to judge which businesses will use the data).
