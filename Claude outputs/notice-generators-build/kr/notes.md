# South Korea: reading notes (12 November 2026)

The Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA, 개인정보 보호법) read in Korean on the National Law
Information Center (law.go.kr, lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=283839, efYd=20260911): the version in force from
11 September 2026, Act No. 21445 of 10 March 2026 (partial amendment). The page also prints amendments
not yet in force, marked "[시행일: 2027. 3. 9.]" (Act of 8 September 2026: Art. 2(9) defines artificial
intelligence technology by reference to the AI Basic Act). Korean is the only authentic text; the notice
wording is a translation.

## Articles read (browser pane, page text)
- Art. 2: personal information (incl. pseudonymised information), processing, data subject, personal
  information controller (개인정보처리자), public institutions.
- Art. 3(5): the controller discloses matters on processing, such as the privacy policy under Art. 30.
- Art. 4: rights of the data subject (information, consent choices, access and transmission, suspension,
  correction, deletion, destruction, redress, refusal of or explanation for fully automated decisions).
- Art. 15(1): collection and use on (1) consent; (2) special provision of law or unavoidable to comply with
  a legal obligation; (3) a public institution's duties; (4) performing a contract with the data subject, or
  steps at their request before one; (5) clearly necessary for urgent life, body or property interests of
  the data subject or a third party; (6) legitimate interests of the controller clearly overriding the data
  subject's rights, related and within a reasonable scope; (7) urgent public safety, such as public health.
  Art. 15(2): when asking consent, inform: (1) purposes; (2) items; (3) retention and use period; (4) the
  right to refuse consent and any disadvantage of refusing; changes need notice and consent again.
  Art. 15(3): use without consent within a scope reasonably related to the original purpose, per decree.
- Art. 16: minimum collection; with consent, tell people specifically that they can refuse consent to
  data beyond the minimum; no refusal of goods or services for that.
- Art. 17(1): provision (including sharing) to a third party on (1) consent or (2) the purposes for which it
  was collected under Art. 15(1)(2), (3), (5) to (7). Art. 17(2): when asking consent, inform: (1) the
  recipient; (2) the recipient's purposes; (3) the items; (4) the recipient's retention and use period;
  (5) the right to refuse and any disadvantage. Art. 17(4): provision without consent within a scope
  reasonably related to the original purpose, per decree.
- Art. 18(1), (2): use or provision beyond the purposes only (1) with separate consent; (2) a special
  provision of another law; (3) urgent life, body, property; (5) to (9) public institutions only; (10)
  urgent public safety; never where it unfairly infringes the interests of the data subject or a third
  party. Art. 18(3): consent items as Art. 17(2). Art. 18(4): public institutions publish in the Official
  Gazette or website. Art. 18(5): limits and safeguards asked of the recipient.
- Art. 20(1): data collected from others: on request, tell the person at once (1) the source, (2) the
  purposes, (3) the right to ask suspension or withdraw consent (Art. 37). Art. 20(2): controllers meeting
  the decree criteria (types, volume, staff, turnover) tell people without request when data is received
  under Art. 17(1)(1) from a third party, unless no contact data. Exceptions (4): files under Art. 32(2);
  risk to others' life, body, property, where clearly overriding.
- Art. 20-2: controllers meeting the decree criteria notify people periodically of the use and provision
  history, or how to check it.
- Art. 21: destruction without delay when unnecessary (retention period ends, purpose achieved,
  pseudonymised retention ends), unless other laws require keeping; then stored separately (3); method and
  procedure by decree (4).
- Art. 22(1): each consent item set out separately and clearly; separate consent for Art. 15(1)(1), 17(1)(1),
  18(2)(1), 19(1), 23(1)(1), 24(1)(1), marketing (7). (2) written consent: important contents clearly marked
  (PIPC notice). (3) data processed without consent: its items and legal basis disclosed separately from
  consent-based data, in the privacy policy (Art. 30(2)) or by email etc.; burden of proof on the controller.
  (5) no refusal of goods or services because optional consent, Art. 18(2)(1) consent or marketing consent
  is refused.
- Art. 22-2: under 14: the legal representative's consent, and confirm it (1); minimum information to get it
  collected from the child (2); notices to under-14s in an easy format and clear, plain language (3).
- Art. 23(1): sensitive information (민감정보): ideology and beliefs, joining or leaving a trade union or
  political party, political views, health, sex life, and other information set by decree that clearly
  threatens privacy; processed only (1) with consent separate from other consents after the Art. 15(2) or
  17(2) information, or (2) where laws require or permit it. (2) security measures under Art. 29. (3) where
  information made public while providing goods or services can reveal sensitive information, tell people
  before providing them that it can become public and how to choose not to make it public.
- Art. 24(1): unique identifiers set by decree (주민등록번호 is further limited by Art. 24-2): only with
  separate consent after the Art. 15(2) or 17(2) information, or where laws specifically require or permit.
  (3) encryption and other safeguards.
- Art. 26: entrustment (업무위탁): (1) a written instrument; (2) the entrusting controller discloses the
  work entrusted and the entrustee (sub-entrustees included) so people can easily check them at any time,
  by the decree method; (3) for promotion or sales work, the work and the entrustee are notified to people,
  and changes too; (4) training and supervision; (6) re-entrustment needs consent of the entrusting
  controller; (8) entrustees are bound by most controller rules.
- Art. 27: business transfer or merger: tell people beforehand of (1) the transfer, (2) the transferee's
  name, address, telephone and other contacts, (3) how to object and the procedure; the transferee tells
  people on receipt unless the transferor did; original purposes only.
- Art. 28-2: pseudonymised information processed without consent for statistics, scientific research and
  archiving in the public interest; no re-identifying information when provided to third parties.
- Art. 28-8(1): transfer abroad (provision, including being viewed from abroad; entrusted processing;
  storage) only if (1) separate consent to the transfer; (2) a law, a treaty or international agreement to
  which Korea is party provides for it; (3) entrusted processing or storage needed to conclude and perform a
  contract with the data subject, where the (2) items are (a) disclosed in the privacy policy or (b)
  notified by email or another decree method; (4) the recipient holds a certification the PIPC designates
  (such as ISMS-P under Art. 32-2) and has taken the safety and rights measures and the measures to apply
  them in the destination country; (5) the PIPC recognises the destination's protection as substantially
  equivalent. Art. 28-8(2): before consent, inform: (1) the items; (2) the country, timing and method of
  transfer; (3) the recipient's name (for a legal person, its name and contact details); (4) the
  recipient's purposes and retention and use period; (5) how to refuse the transfer, the procedure, and the
  effect of refusing. (3) changes: notice and consent. (4) in every case: Arts. 17 to 19 and Chapter 5 apply,
  and the decree safeguards. (5) no contract breaching the Act.
- Art. 28-9: the PIPC can order a transfer stopped.
- Art. 30(1): the privacy policy (개인정보 처리방침) contains: (1) purposes of processing; (2) processing and
  retention periods; (3) provision to third parties (if any); (3-2) destruction procedure and method, and,
  where data is kept under Art. 21(1) proviso, the legal ground and the items kept; (3-3) the Art. 23(3)
  possibility of sensitive information being made public and how to opt out (if any); (4) entrustment (if
  any); (4-2) pseudonymised information under Arts. 28-2 and 28-3 (if any); (5) rights and duties of data
  subjects and legal representatives, and how to exercise them; (6) the CPO's name, or the name and telephone
  and other contacts of the department handling privacy work and complaints; (7) installation and operation
  of devices that collect personal information automatically, such as internet access log files (cookies),
  and how to refuse them (if any); (8) other matters set by decree. Public institutions: for the files
  registered under Art. 32. (2) established or changed: published so people can easily check, by the decree
  method. (3) where it differs from a contract, the version more favourable to the data subject applies.
  (4) the PIPC's drafting guidelines (작성지침), recommended.
- Art. 31: the chief privacy officer (개인정보 보호책임자, CPO) is designated; below decree thresholds
  (staff, turnover) the owner or representative is the CPO (2). Large controllers: board resolution and
  report to the PIPC (3). Duties (4) include handling complaints and redress (5).
- Art. 31-2: a domestic representative (국내대리인) for controllers without an address or office in Korea
  that meet the decree criteria (turnover, volume); in writing; it handles complaints and redress, breach
  notices and reports, and document submissions; it must have an address or office in Korea, chosen from a
  Korean subsidiary or controlled company where there is one (2). The privacy policy includes (4): (1) its
  name (for a legal person, the name and the representative's name); (2) its address (place of business),
  telephone and email.
- Art. 35: access (열람); public institutions also through the PIPC (2); within the decree period, with
  postponement for good reason (3); restriction or refusal with reasons (4).
- Art. 35-2: transmission (전송, portability) of the person's data, to themselves (1) or to a personal data
  management institution or a qualifying recipient (2), against controllers meeting the decree criteria;
  data processed on consent (Arts. 15(1)(1), 23(1)(1), 24(1)(1)) or for a contract (15(1)(4)), or designated
  data processed under law; not derived data; computer-processed only.
- Art. 36: correction or deletion after access; no deletion where another law names the data as data to
  collect; result notified.
- Art. 37: suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent; refusal grounds (2): (1) law or legal
  obligation; (2) others' life, body, property; (3) a public institution's duties; (4) a contracted service
  cannot be provided and the person has not clearly ended the contract; reasons notified without delay (4).
  On withdrawal, destroy without delay (3).
- Art. 37-2: automated decisions (fully automated systems, including AI): a right to refuse a decision that
  significantly affects rights or duties, unless made under Art. 15(1)(1), (2) or (4) (consent, law,
  contract) (1); a right to ask for an explanation of any automated decision (2); the controller stops
  applying it or reprocesses with human involvement and explains, unless good reason (3); the controller
  discloses the criteria, procedure and how personal information is processed so people can easily check
  them (4).
- Art. 38: requests (열람등요구: access, transmission, correction or deletion, suspension and withdrawal,
  refusal and explanation) through an agent (1); the legal representative of a child under 14 (2); fees and
  postage by decree (3); the controller sets and publishes the concrete method and procedure, no harder than
  collection (4); and a procedure to object to a refusal, and tells people about it (5).
- Amendments not in force, printed on the page with "[시행일: 2027. 3. 9.]" (Act of 8 September 2026): Art. 2(9)
  (AI technology as defined in the AI Basic Act), Art. 7-9 (PIPC deliberation on AI development uses),
  Arts. 28-12 to 28-15 (use of personal information to develop AI technology, and limits on it; Art. 28-12(5)
  adds the purposes and types to the privacy policy), and a 2027 text of Art. 37-2 that only respaces
  "인공지능 기술" as "인공지능기술" (the current Art. 37-2 is in force; corrected after review K28);
  "[시행일: 2027. 7. 1.]" Art. 32-2(1) proviso, Art. 75(2)(15). Not modelled until in force; row S25 says so.

## Enforcement Decree (개인정보 보호법 시행령)
Read on law.go.kr (lsiSeq=291731, efYd=20261110): Presidential Decree No. 36804 of 10 November 2026, in
force 10 November 2026 (an amendment by another decree).
- Art. 18: sensitive information also covers (1) genetic information from genetic tests; (2) criminal
  record data; (3) biometric information generated by technical means to identify a person from physical,
  physiological or behavioural features; (4) race or ethnicity (public institutions under Art. 18(2)(5) to
  (9) excepted).
- Art. 28(2): the entrustment disclosure (Act Art. 26(2)) is continuously posted on the website; otherwise
  (3) at the place of business, in a newspaper or Official Gazette, in a publication issued twice a year, or
  in the contract. (4) promotion or sales entrustment notified in writing or similar.
- Art. 29-7: notice under Act Art. 28-8(1)(3)(b): in writing or similar (서면등).
- Art. 29-9: PIPC recognition of equivalent protection (Act Art. 28-8(1)(5)), published in the Official
  Gazette and on the PIPC website; may be limited by scope, recipients, period or conditions.
- Art. 30: security measures (internal management plan; access rights; access control; encryption; access
  logs; anti-malware; physical measures; others); details in a PIPC notice.
- Art. 31(1): the privacy policy also contains: (1) the items of personal information processed; (2) for
  transfers abroad under Act Art. 28-8(1), the legal ground of the transfer and the Art. 28-8(2) items; (3)
  the security measures under Decree Art. 30; (4) where the controller collects directly from data
  subjects in Korea from abroad, the name of the country where it processes the information. (2) posted
  continuously on the website; (3) otherwise at the place of business, in a newspaper or Official Gazette,
  a publication issued twice a year, or the contract.
- Art. 14-2: further use or provision without consent (Act Arts. 15(3), 17(4)): consider (1) relation to the
  original purpose; (2) foreseeability from the context of collection or practice; (3) no unfair
  infringement of the data subject's interests; (4) pseudonymisation or encryption. (2) where it happens
  continuously, publish the criteria for these in the privacy policy, and the CPO checks them.
- Art. 15-2: Act Art. 20(2) applies to controllers processing sensitive or unique identifying information
  of 50,000 or more people, or personal information of 1,000,000 or more (daily average over the last three
  months of the previous year); notice within three months of receipt, in writing, email, phone, text or an
  in-service notification.
- Art. 17-2: ways to confirm a legal representative's consent (text message confirmation, card data, mobile
  identity check, signed paper, email, telephone, similar); minimum information from the child: the legal
  representative's name and contact details.
- Art. 32(1): the CPO need not be designated by a small business owner (소상공인); (2) the CPO is the owner or
  representative, or an officer (or the head of the privacy department where there are no officers);
  (3) large controllers (turnover over KRW 180 billion and sensitive or identifying data of 50,000 or more
  people, or personal data of 1,000,000 or more; schools by enrolment; others) meet qualifications.
- Art. 32-3: a domestic representative is required for a controller without an address or office in Korea
  with (1) total turnover of KRW 1 trillion or more in the previous year, (2) data of 1,000,000 or more data
  subjects in Korea stored (daily average over the last three months of the previous year), or (3) a PIPC
  decision after a document request.
- Art. 41: access within 10 days (4); request methods no harder than collection, in writing, phone, email,
  internet; at least the same channel as collection; a website operator publishes the method on its website.
- Art. 44-4: automated decision disclosure on the website (or in writing beforehand where there is no
  website): (1) that automated decisions are made, their purpose and the data subjects concerned; (2) the
  main types of personal information used and their relation to the decision; (3) the factors considered
  and how the main personal information is processed; (4) where sensitive or under-14 data is processed, its
  purpose and the items; (5) the right to refuse or ask for an explanation, and how. Standard terms, visual
  aids allowed.
- Art. 16: destruction: electronic files permanently deleted so they cannot be restored (or anonymised where
  deletion is technically very hard); other media shredded or burnt.
- Art. 17: consent conditions (free, specific and clear, easy wording, clear way to indicate) and methods
  (signed paper, telephone, website, email, similar); (3) the important contents marked in written consent:
  contacting for promotion or sales; sensitive information; passport, driving licence and alien registration
  numbers; retention periods (the recipient's for provision); the recipient and its purposes; (4) make
  clear that the person can choose; (5) "in writing or similar" means writing, email, fax, telephone, text
  message or similar.
- Art. 29-10: for every transfer abroad: security measures, complaint handling and dispute resolution, other
  measures, agreed with the recipient beforehand and put in the contract.
- Art. 44-2: refusal and explanation or review requests follow the method the controller publishes under
  Art. 44-4(1)(5); the person can ask (1) an explanation of the criteria and process, (2) a review of whether
  their opinion (such as added information) can be reflected.
- Art. 47: fees and postage within actual cost; none where the controller caused the request; paid as the
  controller sets (private controllers).

## PIPC guidance
- PIPC 「개인정보 처리방침 작성지침」, revised April 2026 (pipc.go.kr notice of 23 April 2026; privacy.go.kr
  "2026 개인정보 처리방침 작성지침"). The PIPC press release of 24 April 2026 (pipc.go.kr nttId=12021, read
  through a web fetch) lists the changes: recipients and entrustees that are numerous or change often may be
  listed by type, with how to check the actual ones; changes that significantly affect rights announced
  before or at once when the policy changes, minor changes (entrustee lists) batched over a period such as
  four weeks; a privacy policy is needed where any data is stored on a server even with on-device features
  (on-device only: say so and the deletion criteria, recommended); entrustees' own policies focus on rights;
  examples for behavioural information; the simplified policy lists the required items (items, purposes,
  retention, third parties and entrustment, how to exercise rights); a generative AI appendix (inputs and
  outputs as processed items, a warning against entering sensitive or identifying information, whether
  inputs train models and the opt-out, how to report inappropriate answers). The 2025 revision separated
  legal requirements from recommendations, allowed types where listing every item is impracticable, the
  contact of the department handling complaints, and publication in a service menu, settings, sign-up or
  login area, not only the foot of the first page (news reports of 21 April 2025). The guideline is not law:
  its points are good practice rows.
- Read the 2026 guideline itself (185 pages; privacy.go.kr PDF, text extracted with pdf.js in the browser
  pane): basic principles p. 12 (comply with Act Art. 30(1) and Decree Art. 31(1); accurate and current;
  each statutory item explicitly separated, plain words (Standard Guidelines Art. 18(1)); a foreign business
  subject to the Act writes the policy in Korean; rights procedures no harder than collection); p. 14 to 15:
  the 24 items, marked required, "if applicable" (해당시) or "recommended" (권장): 1 title and preamble; 2
  purposes; 3 items processed; 4 children under 14 (if applicable); 5 processing and retention periods; 6
  destruction; 7 third parties (if applicable); 8 criteria for continuous further use or provision (if
  applicable); 9 entrustment (if applicable); 10 collection from abroad and transfers abroad (if applicable);
  11 security measures; 12 sensitive information possibly made public (if applicable); 13 pseudonymised
  information (if applicable); 14 automatic collection devices and refusal (if applicable); 15 third parties
  collecting behavioural information through such devices (recommended, if applicable); 16 rights and duties
  of data subjects and legal representatives, and how to exercise them; 17 automated decisions (if
  applicable); 18 CPO or department, and the complaint-handling department; 19 domestic representative (if
  applicable); 20 remedies for infringement (recommended); 21, 22 fixed and mobile video devices (if
  applicable); 23 voluntary items (recommended); 24 changes to the policy. Where an item does not apply, the
  policy can say so. Standard Guidelines Art. 18(2): state that the data processed is the minimum necessary.
  Recommended: compare before and after when the policy changes.
- Guideline item 3 (p. 24): items processed (Decree Art. 31(1)(1)), specific, not "etc."; types allowed
  where items are many, with the items per type; data processed with consent and without consent listed
  separately; for data processed without consent, the items and the legal basis; where a specific law is
  the basis, the law and the article.
- Item 10 (pp. 45 to 46): collection from abroad and transfers abroad set apart from provision and
  entrustment; a business collecting directly from abroad (subject to the Act directly) names every country
  where it processes the data; for transfers (provision, viewing, entrusted processing, storage; onward
  transfers included; every country of a cloud), the legal basis under Art. 28-8(1) and the Art. 28-8(2)
  items; a law, treaty or agreement named with its article; a transfer that is provision or entrustment can
  be written here once, saying which it is and including the third party or entrustment items.
- Item 20 (p. 75, recommended): remedies: the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972,
  www.kopico.go.kr), the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr), the
  police (182, ecrm.police.go.kr), with current details.
- Item 24 (p. 83): changes: the date of application and the change published continuously; earlier versions
  published with their periods; changes notified by a notice or pop-up (recommended); significant changes
  (items, purposes) separately notified before or at once, with a comparison table; minor frequent changes
  (entrustee lists) batched over a reasonable period such as four weeks, with start and end dates.
- Item 1 (p. 20): the standard title "개인정보 처리방침" (Privacy Policy) at the top, the controller's name in
  the title recommended; a preamble naming the controller and the services covered recommended.
- Item 4 (p. 29): under 14: the data processed to get the legal representative's consent (name, mobile
  number); recommended: that data is collected with the legal representative's consent and how consent is
  confirmed (the method actually used); plain language; a separate children's policy where children are the
  main users.
- Item 18 (p. 72): the CPO's name, or the department for privacy work and complaints, with telephone and
  other contacts; telephone and email both recommended; the CPO's name and the department both recommended;
  contacts must work in practice.
- Item 19 (p. 73): the domestic representative's name (for a legal person: its name and representative's
  name), address (place of business), telephone (reachable in Korea) and email; omission: a fine of up to
  KRW 10 million.
- Item 5 (p. 31): processing and retention periods per purpose, matching the consent notice; where a law sets
  the period, the law and the period (with the items, recommended); concrete periods, or the criteria where
  a period cannot be fixed.
- Item 16 (p. 65): the rights of data subjects and legal representatives (access, transmission, correction
  and deletion, suspension, withdrawal of consent, refusal and explanation of automated decisions) and how to
  exercise them concretely, matching practice and no harder than collection; website functions explained;
  the department and contact for requests recommended; transmitting controllers describe how to use the
  transmission right.
- Item 17 (p. 67): automated decisions: the five Decree Art. 44-4(1) items.
- Item 6 (p. 35): destruction without delay when unnecessary; data kept under other laws: the legal basis,
  the items and the period (can be given under item 5 with a pointer); data kept apart; the procedure and
  method in detail.
- Item 7 (p. 37): third parties: with consent: the legal basis (recommended), the recipient, its purposes,
  the items, its retention period; without consent: the legal basis, the recipient, its purposes, the items
  (the legal retention period recommended). Recipients named one by one, never "etc." or "N companies" or
  categories such as "financial institutions" (the 2026 revision allows types where they are numerous or
  change often, with how to check the actual ones).
- Item 9 (p. 42): entrustment: the entrustee (sub-entrustees included) and the work entrusted, named, not
  "etc."; many entrustees: a pop-up, a downloadable list or a page; or where the person can see them in the
  service (order history), the path, the work and the type of entrustee; entrustment abroad may be written
  under item 10.
- Item 14 (p. 54): cookies and similar technologies: what they are, the purposes, how data is collected,
  how to refuse (browser settings); behavioural information processed identifiably: its collection, use,
  provision and refusal.

## Amendment history (law.go.kr, 전체 제정·개정이유, read in the browser pane)
- Act No. 21910 (8 September 2026, in force 9 March 2027): previously lawfully collected personal information
  may be used to develop AI technology where anonymisation or pseudonymisation cannot achieve it, for the
  public interest and other conditions, after PIPC deliberation; prior risk assessment. Not modelled.
- Act No. 21445 (10 March 2026, in force 11 September 2026): the owner or representative is the final
  responsible person (Art. 30-3); CPO designation reported for decree-level controllers, CPO powers
  strengthened (Art. 31); certification mandatory above decree thresholds (Art. 32-2(1) proviso, from
  1 July 2027); breach notice extended to forgery, alteration and damage, and to possible breaches (Art.
  34); higher fines for repeated or large breaches (Art. 64-2). No change to the privacy policy items.
- Act No. 20897 (1 April 2025, in force 2 October 2025): domestic representative chosen from a Korean
  subsidiary or controlled company; supervision; the privacy policy must include its name, address,
  telephone and email (fine up to KRW 10 million).
- Act No. 19234 (14 March 2023, in force 15 September 2023): transmission right, automated decisions,
  one set of rules for online and offline, wider transfer bases.

## What the notice therefore needs (Part S)
The privacy policy (개인정보 처리방침, Art. 30; Decree Art. 31), titled as such, published continuously on
the website: purposes; items processed per purpose, split into data processed with consent and data
processed without consent with its legal basis (Art. 22(3)); processing and retention periods (laws
named where they set them); destruction procedure and method, with data kept under other laws; third
parties (recipient by name, its purposes, items, retention, basis); entrustment (entrustee, work); for
transfers abroad the basis under Art. 28-8(1) and the Art. 28-8(2) items, and for a business collecting
directly from abroad the countries where it processes; security measures; sensitive information that can
become public, if any; pseudonymised information, if any; cookies and other automatic collection and how
to refuse; rights of data subjects and legal representatives (access, transmission where the controller is
a designated transmitter, correction and deletion, suspension, withdrawal of consent, refusal and
explanation of automated decisions) and how to exercise them, with the objection procedure (Art. 38(5));
automated decisions (Decree Art. 44-4); the CPO's name or the department and its contacts; a domestic
representative where required; children under 14 (legal representative's consent); criteria for continuous
further use or provision (Decree Art. 14-2(2)); remedies (recommended); changes with earlier versions.
Korean is expected; a foreign business subject to the Act writes the policy in Korean (guideline p. 12).
Not modelled: public institutions' registered files and Art. 18(2)(5) to (9); fixed and mobile video
devices (Arts. 25, 25-2); the 2027 AI development provisions.
- PIPC equivalence recognition (Art. 28-8(1)(5)): the European Union, announced 16 September 2025 (law firm
  and news reports read through search results: lawtimes.co.kr, byline.network; the PIPC's own notice not
  read); the United Kingdom under review since autumn 2026 (mt.co.kr, 1 October 2026). Art. 28-11: onward
  transfers by a recipient abroad follow the transfer rules. The PIPC's transfer page (pipc.go.kr
  D060040000, search extract): the transfer basis, and for consent the consent contents, go in the privacy
  policy (Decree Art. 31(1)(2)).
