GDPR Fine Calculator

Supervisory authorities do not publish a formula. What they publish is decisions. This page takes the published decisions where the fined organisation’s turnover is known, finds the ones closest to your own size and circumstance, and shows what those organisations actually paid as a proportion of turnover. It is a benchmark drawn from enforcement practice, not a prediction and not a legal opinion.

Nothing on this page is transmitted. No figure you enter leaves your browser, and the page contacts no server for anything: all __ROWCOUNT__ decisions are inside the file.

This is not legal advice and not a prediction of what any supervisory authority would do.
