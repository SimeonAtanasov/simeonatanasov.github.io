Cookie Banner Scanner

Checks whether websites show a cookie banner before cookies are created. The scanner focuses on OneTrust detection, banner visibility, cookies observed, and the risk of cookies being dropped without a banner.

Goals: Detect OneTrust as the primary consent banner, inspect cookies dropped during initial page load, highlight the highest-risk compliance scenario (no banner detected while cookies are dropped), and map observed cookies to category risk levels.
